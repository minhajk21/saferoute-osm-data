**How these files were made (ODbL 4.6).** Each release names, in its notes and in `manifest.json`, the commit of
the scripts that built it. Those scripts (`pipeline/`), with the Geofabrik extracts listed in `manifest.json`
(each with its URL, size, MD5, SHA-256 and OpenStreetMap timestamp), reproduce the files.
